FAQ

If you have another question, write to us and we'll get back to you.

Why does management need training?

NIS2 requires management bodies to approve the measures, oversee their implementation and receive training. Besides, the key decisions in a crisis are made by management.

How much time does it take?

A 2-to-4-hour session is enough to start; the crisis exercise takes about 4 hours more.

Is it confidential?

Yes. The sessions and exercises are handled in full confidentiality.
